Questions families ask first

Quick answers

Anything else? That's what the call is for.

Is the call really free, and do I have to apply afterwards?

Yes, it's free and there's no obligation. Jan's goal is to help you decide, and he'll tell you if another route suits you better.

How long does a bachelor's degree take?

Tio's fast-track bachelor's takes 3 years (180 EC), including a 20-week internship. You can start in September or January.

What's the difference between Business Management and International Business Management?

Business Management focuses on business administration: marketing, finance, processes, management and strategy. International Business Management focuses on business and entrepreneurship across countries and cultures.

What qualifications do I need?

A diploma equivalent to the required Dutch secondary-education level, plus proof of English proficiency. Accepted qualifications depend on your education system, so Jan can help you check yours.

How much does it cost, and are there scholarships?

Bachelor's tuition is €24,500 a year plus a €95 enrolment fee, payable in one payment or in instalments. The Talent (25%) and Super Talent (50%) scholarships reduce annual tuition for students with excellent results.

Is housing really guaranteed?

Yes. Housing is guaranteed for the full duration of your programme, furnished and ready in Amsterdam, Utrecht, Rotterdam or Eindhoven. Options include city apartments, suburban living and heritage housing.

What support do international students get?

Visa support, insurance, airport pick-up, a dedicated study coach and a student buddy. Tio reports a visa acceptance rate of over 99%, though no visa can be guaranteed.

Can I stay in the Netherlands after graduating?

Graduates can apply for the Orientation Year: 12 extra months to work or start a business in the Netherlands.
