Frequently asked questions

Which bachelor's programmes can I choose from?

International Business Management, Business Management, Commerce Economics and Entrepreneurship, Hotel and Event Management, and International Tourism Management.

What qualifications do I need?

A diploma equivalent to the required Dutch secondary-education level, plus English proficiency. Jan can tell you how your diploma and test are likely to be assessed; Admissions confirms.

What does it cost?

Bachelor's tuition is €24,500 a year plus €95 enrolment. Tuition can be paid in one payment or in 12 instalments. Talent (25%) and Super Talent (50%) scholarships are available for excellent results.

Is housing really guaranteed?

Yes, for the full duration of your programme: Tio-Tower apartments, Cityscape studios, Heritage housing or Suburban Living, in Amsterdam, Utrecht, Rotterdam or Eindhoven.

Is a visa guaranteed?

No school can guarantee a visa. Tio provides visa support and reports a visa acceptance rate of over 99%.
